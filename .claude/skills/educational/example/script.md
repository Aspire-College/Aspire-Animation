# Study smarter

Skill: `educational` v1.0.0 · Engine: `p5` · Date: 2026-10-30

The reference reel for the educational series: it shows the shape of a kinetic-type `reel.js` (the kit, anchors, moves, the CTA end screen). The script is a demo written for it, not a client script.

## Script

0:00–0:02.8 Reading it *once* isn’t learning.
0:02.8–0:05.6 Test yourself *tomorrow.*
0:05.6–0:08.6 Then again in *3 days.*
0:08.6–0:10.6 Save this for *exam week.* (CTA, end-screen hold 2 s)

## Brief

Students before exams. Series look: paper ground, ink type, accent orange, Archivo Black. No caption block: the words are the picture.

## Plan

- Scenes: the claim (slam, underline on "learning."), a calendar row with day 2 lit (rise, boxed "tomorrow."), the same row moved on to day 5 with an arc hop (mask, marker on "3 days."), the end screen with a bookmark that breathes and fills on the tap.
- Moves: **rise** (the paper scrolls up onto the calendar), **along** (the calendar row slides on three days), **fall** (down onto the end screen, carrying the calendar).
- Sound: a low room bed to the end; thump and impact on the slam, scribble on the underline, riser into the calendar, click and pop on day 2, whoosh along, ticking under the hop and a ding on day 5, whoosh down, thump, click and chime on the tap.
